Privacy Policy

Zero Day Devs built this game as an indie developer. This game is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is. This page informs players about our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information if anyone decides to play our game.

Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personal information directly. However, our game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect certain information, such as:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID)
  • Interaction with ads
  • General location (for ad targeting)
  • Usage data

This information is used by Google to serve relevant ads and improve their ad services. For more details on how AdMob handles user data, please visit: Google AdMob Privacy Policy

In-App Purchases

Our game offers optional in-app purchases, such as boosters, to enhance gameplay. Purchases are handled securely through the Google Play billing system.

We do not collect or store any payment information (such as credit/debit card details). All transactions and payment data are managed directly by Google Play in accordance with their policies.

For more details, please review: Google Play Terms of Service and Google Payments Privacy Notice

Children’s Privacy

Our game is rated PEGI 3, meaning it is suitable for all age groups. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take necessary action.

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal information with anyone. All data collected through ads is managed by Google in accordance with their policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
