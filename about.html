About Zero Day Devs

Zero Day Devs is an independent game development studio passionate about creating unique and challenging games. Our team draws inspiration from iconic games, especially FromSoftware's unforgettable boss battles and immersive worlds.

Our mission is simple: craft engaging, memorable experiences that challenge the player and spark creativity. Each project is designed to push the boundaries of indie game development while keeping gameplay fun and rewarding.

With years of experience and an undying passion for gaming, we focus on quality, innovation, and attention to detail. We aim to deliver games that are not only enjoyable but also leave a lasting impression on players around the world.
